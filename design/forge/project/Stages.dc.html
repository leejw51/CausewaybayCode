Six stages, one robot

1 · HEATING THE FORGE

Planning the pieces, checking disk space. The furnace glows up from cold; no robot yet. Bar: an indeterminate ember shimmer.

2 · HAMMERING THE WEIGHTS

Downloading. Parts appear with the percent: feet, legs, torso, arms, head, face plate. Each arrives red-hot, the hammer strikes with sparks, it cools to steel.

3 · TEMPERING

Verifying every piece against its fingerprint. A cyan scanning beam sweeps the robot top to bottom; the hammer rests.

4 · WAKING IT UP

Loading into memory. The core pulses, the eyes flicker on and brighten. No numbers to wait on: a calm pulse, about ten seconds.

5 · READY

Eyes full cyan, it waves, one burst of sparks. After 1.5 s the scene fades and the chat composer takes focus.

THE FORGE WENT COLD

Failed. The robot sags, embers dim. The error in plain words and a Try again button; what is done is kept.
